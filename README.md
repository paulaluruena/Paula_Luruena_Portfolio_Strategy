# Paula Luruena Marquez — Strategy Consulting Portfolio

A clean, business-focused portfolio for roles in **people analytics, talent, HR, and organizational development**. It maps Paula’s experience to core I/O competencies, including employee feedback and retention, interviewing and assessment, research methods and data, behavior and performance, training, leadership, and cross-cultural communication.

Tailored copy of Paula’s I/O psychology portfolio for analyst roles in **functional and operations strategy**. It adds a **Role fit** section that maps the work of a strategy analyst to her experience, reframes her interests around human capital, AI and the future of work, and customer strategy, and closes the “Why psychology” page with how psychology connects to strategy consulting.

Related portfolios: `Paula_Luruena_Professional_Portfolio_IOpsych`, `Paula-Luruena-Professional-Portfolio_AIxMH`, and `paula-luruena-portfolio-retail`.

## Content

- **Overview:** name, focus, and key facts, with a side rail for navigation and contact.
- **Role fit:** the core work of a strategy analyst, each part linked to the experience that supports it.
- **Profile:** a short statement and bio, with psychology and service to others at the center.
- **Why psychology (`story.html`):** an essay-style origin story in five parts, weaving in the psychologists whose ideas shaped her thinking, with a reading progress bar and a final part that connects psychology to this portfolio’s focus.
- **Interests:** expanding panels for all three of her interest areas, with this portfolio’s main focus first.
- **Experience:** a filterable explorer of every role, each with a timeline and a short takeaway. Roles most relevant to this portfolio are tagged.
- **Background:** education, languages, community, and the roles she is pursuing.

Professional facts come from Paula’s CV and résumé. Focus areas are labeled as interests, clinical observation is described as observational, and skills in development are labeled that way. Street address and phone number are intentionally left off the public site.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or run `npm ci` then `npm run dev`.

## Edit

All three portfolios share one design and the same content; each only shifts the emphasis toward its main focus.

- `index.html` — portfolio page
- `story.html` — the “Why psychology” page
- `styles.css` — shared design: Bodoni Moda and Hanken Grotesk, a side rail with live New Orleans and Madrid clocks, and wine accents
- `script.js` — expanding focus panels, filterable experience explorer with a 2021–2027 timeline, competency matrix filters, copy-email button, and mobile menu
- `assets/` — small portrait, local fonts with licenses, and the downloadable résumé

Animations are disabled for people who prefer reduced motion, and the content stays readable without JavaScript.
